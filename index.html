CASE STUDY 03

AI-Driven Platform Efficiency & Cost Optimization

Cloud Efficiency + Workflow Simplification + AI Investment Enablement
12–18 months $5M+ Impact Platform & Finance Enterprise-Wide
AWS S3 Databricks Informatica BDM Governance

The Challenge

  • Rapid growth of platform usage led to uncontrolled cloud storage costs
  • Years of unmaintained data (logs, unused files) created 3 petabytes of excess storage
  • Inefficient legacy workflows (Informatica BDM) increased engineering cost and complexity
  • Limited visibility and governance over platform usage constrained optimization

The Solution

  • Identified root causes of inefficiency and implemented data lifecycle governance (vacuum cycles, retention policies)
  • Led cross-functional alignment across platform, finance, and engineering
  • Simplified Informatica workflows through vendor collaboration, reducing engineering effort and migration complexity
  • Established repeatable governance for sustained efficiency
